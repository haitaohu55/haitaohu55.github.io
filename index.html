About me

I am a Ph.D. student interested in theoretical and computational condensed matter physics, including Anderson localization, topological insulators, and low-dimensional materials. I mainly use density functional theory and tight-binding models in my research.

Research interests

  • Anderson localization in quasiperiodic systems
  • Electronic structure and band topology of 2D materials
