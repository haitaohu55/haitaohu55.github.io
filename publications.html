Hidden Self Duality and Exact Mobility Edges in Quasiperiodic Network Models

Hai-Tao Hu, Xiaoshui Lin, Ai-Min Guo, Guangcan Guo, Zijing Lin, and Ming Gong

Physical Review Letters 134, 246301 · Published 18 June 2025

Abstract

This work identifies hidden self-duality in quasiperiodic networks that combine periodic and quasiperiodic sites. Eliminating the periodic sites reveals self-dual effective Hamiltonians and exact mobility edges, even when the original models do not appear self-dual. The framework explains the mosaic model, relates resonant states to the shape of its mobility edges, and extends to several network geometries, including non-Hermitian systems. Optical and acoustic waveguide arrays are proposed as possible experimental platforms.

Exact mobility edges in quasiperiodic network models with slowly varying potentials

Hai-Tao Hu, Yang Chen, Xiaoshui Lin, Ai-Min Guo, Zijing Lin, and Ming Gong

Physical Review B 112, 054201 · Published 4 August 2025

Abstract

This paper develops quasiperiodic network models with slowly varying potentials whose mobility edges can be determined exactly without relying on hidden self-duality. For mosaic systems, eliminating the periodic sites produces an effective Hamiltonian with an energy-dependent potential and eigenenergy; the method then extends to more complex network geometries and non-Hermitian cases. Optical-waveguide realizations are proposed, with the associated Anderson transitions expected to be visible in arrays containing roughly 50–100 sites.

Divergent density of states and nonanalytic Lyapunov exponent in one-dimensional slowly varying systems

Hai-Tao Hu, Ming Gong, Guangcan Guo, and Zijing Lin

Physical Review B 112, 245134 · Published 15 December 2025

Abstract

This work shows that a Lyapunov exponent can become nonanalytic within a localized phase, rather than only at the boundary between extended and localized states. The effect appears when localization centers are inhomogeneous along the chain and several localization modes coexist. Across multiple one-dimensional slowly varying models, the resulting nonanalyticity is connected through the Thouless formula to singular behavior in the density of states, revealing finer structure inside the localized phase.
